Context · 2022

Where the idea came from

In 2022 the income tax statement (informe de rendimentos) was not available in the app. To file their income tax, customers depended on the branch.

01

Go to the branch or call the account manager

The request only moved forward with human assistance.

02

Wait for the export

The manager generated the document and sent it back to the customer.

03

Receive the statement

Each customer received it however the manager chose to send it.

04

Forward it to the accountant

Only then did the document reach the person who would use it.

An outdated process for a document customers need every year.

Context · up to 2022

The landscape up to 2022

36.3 M

income tax returns received by the Receita Federal in 2022 (base year 2021).

43%

of US core banking systems ran on COBOL, per a 2017 estimate cited by Reuters.

No public figure

How many statements each bank issued through service channels is not public data. I used the national tax-return total as a scale reference.

The bottlenecks

Legacy systems

The core was coded in older languages such as COBOL, common in large banks. Changing it took specialized programming.

Development queue

Every new feature competed for priority in the IT queue, so the statement stayed with human service.

No AI in the workflow

Prototyping, testing and coding were slow, separate stages, with no tools connecting them.

Sources: Receita Federal (IRPF 2022); Reuters (2017), cited by DXC. Market data, not Bradesco data.

Discover

Research and benchmark

Who we listened to

The managers who exported income tax statements for customers. They received the request, generated the document and sent it back.

What we wanted to understand

How the request arrived, how much effort it took and what customers did with the document afterwards.

What we looked at in the benchmark

Where the statement lives in other apps, how many steps to the file, the delivered format and how to share it.

What we took into the project

A short path from the home screen, a single PDF file and direct sharing.

Define

Hypotheses

H1

Autonomy

If the statement lives inside the account in the app, customers no longer depend on the branch or the manager.

H2

Discovery

If everyone gets a notification showing the way, the question “where is this feature?” goes away.

H3

Single file

If the statements come out as a single PDF, forwarding them to the accountant becomes simple.

H4

Availability

If the feature is available all year, customers export whenever they need, not only during tax season.

Deliver

Testing with managers

The journey was tested with Bradesco managers, the team that exported the statements, in rounds with different squads and about 5 participants each.

“Where will the statement feature be?”

A simple journey from the home screen and a notification to all users showing the way.

“Will it be available all year?”

Yes. Customers export and forward it to their accountant whenever they need.

The illustration caused confusion

At first it left people unsure. It was the main adjustment after the tests.

Using the flow itself raised no doubts.

Iteration after testing

The income tax lion

The first illustration was confusing. With the team, we brought in the lion, the symbol of income tax in Brazil.

Fewer elements

The illustration was simplified to be understood at a glance.

Waiting screen

The lion shows up while the app fetches and generates the statements, as a loading state.

Simple animation

Subtle motion, just to show that something is happening.

What changed

Before × after

Before

  • Depended on the branch or the account manager
  • Limited to business hours
  • Waiting for someone to export the document
  • The customer was the go-between for bank and accountant

After

  • Right in the account, inside the app
  • Available at any time
  • Statement generated in a few taps
  • A single PDF, ready to save or send to the accountant
The solution

Income tax statements inside the app

The customer finds the shortcut on the home screen, picks the year, generates a single file and shares it.

Benefits

Why it makes things easier

Autonomy

Customers do it themselves, with no need for a branch or manager.

Speed

From tapping the shortcut to the PDF in a few steps.

A single file

The selected statements are merged into one PDF.

History

Statements from the last 5 years available in one place.

Ready for the accountant

The file goes from the app straight to email, messages or files.

Less demand on branches

Export requests no longer take up service time.

Journey

End-to-end journey

From the notification that the statement is available to the saved file and the experience rating.

01Discovery
User action
Gets the notification announcing income tax statements in the app.
Thinking / feeling
“I need this to file my taxes. Where do I find it?”
Design opportunity
Contextual push during tax season, leading straight to the feature.
02Access
User action
Opens the app and signs in.
Thinking / feeling
“I want to get this done quickly.”
Design opportunity
Keep the notification destination after sign-in.
03Entry
User action
Taps “Informe de rendimentos” in Quick access.
Thinking / feeling
“Found it without digging through menus.”
Design opportunity
Featured shortcut on the home screen during tax season.
04Lookup
User action
Waits while the available statements are fetched.
Thinking / feeling
“Did it freeze?”
Design opportunity
Illustration and a time expectation (up to 30 s) ease the wait.
05Selection
User action
Picks the year and which statements to merge.
Thinking / feeling
“I only need 2021, for my account.”
Design opportunity
Year chips, a pre-selected item and help for statements from other products.
06Generation
User action
Waits while everything is merged into one file.
Thinking / feeling
“A single file is easier to send to my accountant.”
Design opportunity
Progress feedback in a friendly tone.
07Delivery
User action
Saves or shares the generated PDF.
Thinking / feeling
“Done, that was it.”
Design opportunity
Clear next steps: save, generate others, back to home.
08Rating
User action
Rates the experience with stars and an optional comment.
Thinking / feeling
“That was easier than I expected.”
Design opportunity
Satisfaction survey at the end of the flow to improve the service.

What I would improve today

Year and statement selection

Years always visible

All five years fit on screen, with no last chip cut off by scrolling.

Statement as a card

Type, status and selection sit together in one larger, easier-to-tap element.

Summary before generating

Shows how many statements are selected and that a single PDF will be generated.

Quieter help

The other-statements block stays collapsed, with less weight and still reachable.

Action pinned at the bottom

The Generate button is always within reach, not tied to the end of the page.

What I would improve today

Success metrics

In 2022 validation was qualitative. Today I would define the indicators before launch and instrument the flow from the first version.

Flow completion

Share of people who reach the PDF among those who open the shortcut.

Time to PDF

From tapping the shortcut to the generated file.

Errors and retries

Failures in lookup and generation, and how often users retry.

Adoption by channel

Statements generated in the app versus requests made at the branch.

Support contacts

Statement requests that reach customer service.

Satisfaction

Star ratings and comments collected at the end of the flow.

These are proposed indicators, not results. Targets depend on baseline data.

What I would improve today

From prototype to code, faster

The project took about 7 months, with many internal validation stages. Today I would shorten the cycle by connecting Claude Code, Codex or another LLM to the pipeline.

1

Research

The LLM helps synthesize interviews and benchmarks into hypotheses.

2

Prototype

Screens and components built in Figma in hours, not weeks.

3

Testing

A clickable prototype and test scripts ready to run with users.

4

Code

The flow prototyped in code, with human review at each delivery.

5

Internal validation

Documentation and evidence ready, with fewer back-and-forths.

Internal validation is still necessary, especially in a bank. The gain is reaching it with a tested prototype and less rework.
